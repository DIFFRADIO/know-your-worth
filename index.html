Why it's higher than you expect

You don't get paid for every day of the year. Holidays, sick days, admin and finding work all take time, and your rate has to cover them.

That's not greedy. It's what it costs to keep doing your work.

Using your rate

  • When someone asks your rate, say the number, then stop. No apology needed.
  • If their budget is lower, offer less work, not a lower rate: "For that budget, I can do a half day."
  • Check your rate every year, or when your costs change.
