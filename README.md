# Know your worth

A freelance day rate calculator for creative freelancers, by Urbanstamp · The Green Room.

Five short steps to a day rate that actually pays your bills. Everything runs in the browser in a single `index.html`, and your numbers are saved only in your own browser.

Live site: https://diffradio.github.io/know-your-worth/

This is a starting point, not financial or tax advice.
